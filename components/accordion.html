How do I create a new project?

Open the Projects area, select New project, add the required details and confirm to create the workspace.

Can I archive or restore a project?

Archived projects remain available to authorized team members and can be restored when needed.

How do team permissions work?

Permissions determine which projects and actions are available to each role.

Where can I find uploaded files?

Files are available from the Files section inside each project.
